# Tungning: The Crown Across the Strait

A Victoria 3 1.15 mod about the survival of the Zheng kingdom. In 1836, Tungning controls Taiwan and the former Lanfang settlement in West Borneo. Its Catholic court has a fleet and a small industrial base to defend, debts to pay, and a trading network that now faces stronger competition.

See [storyline.md](storyline.md) for the alternate history from Koxinga to Daoguang and the [law plan](design/law-plan.md) for the starting laws and later reforms.

Enable **Tungning: The Crown Across the Strait** in a playset and start a **new 1836 campaign**. Select Tungning if it appears in the country picker, or select Great Qing and choose **Play as the Kingdom of Tungning** in the opening event. A Qing player can also stay with Qing.

The Busan outpost is secret in the story. The game displays it as a treaty port, so it follows normal rules for rank and market access. Under the Manila Compact, Tungning gives Spain trade privileges and ships five porcelain per week in return for military assistance. Investment rights are included when the relevant DLC feature is available.

Joseon starts with Yangban Administration and level 1 Confucian Academies. Đại Nam, Qing, and Tungning start with Standardized Examinations. Qing’s Banner Precedence cuts its conscription rate by 50% until the Taiping play starts. Tungning starts with Professional Army and receives +50% conscription from the Port and Village Registers until it registers its first mainland administration. It also starts with level 1 Religious Schools; the Tungning School Charter cancels their conversion bonus. Under the Huế Supply Accord, Đại Nam grants Tungning trade privileges in exchange for two frigates and five artillery per week. Taiwan has a level 3 fishing wharf, a level 3 logging camp producing wood and hardwood, two glassworks, a tooling workshop, a cannon foundry, and a coal mine.

Qing begins with an embargo on Tungning and a small Catholic minority in Fujian. Every six months, Fujian, Guangdong, and Zhejiang can see departures. Unemployment and turmoil raise the odds; Zhejiang and prosperous, calm provinces have lower odds. Taiwan is favored for Fujianese departures, but eligible ports around the Pacific and in the Americas can also receive migrants. Under Closed Borders, a limited group can slip out. When the borders permit migration, the event can start a normal mass migration for the province’s main culture. Victoria 3 draws those migrants from the source country as a whole. After a departure, the originating province has a four-year cooldown.

**The Claim Across the Strait** opens in 1836. The council can hear the Ming memorials or the Strait merchants, and each hearing opens an event for the court to answer. Reasserting the claim opens the mainland campaign. Renouncing it first tests whether Qing will accept a tributary and trade settlement; if the Taiping Rebellion starts while the claim is unsettled, the court faces a different choice.

**The Southern Passage** opens after the first week if Tungning still holds an opening grant and has a Dutch rival. The court can lower export tariffs to buy time, sustain a naval demonstration, or enforce a qualifying demand in Indonesia. If the dispute ends in concessions, only the tracked West Borneo parcels can change hands.

**The Manila Question** gives Tungning eight years from the moment an independent Philippines holds Manila. Offer protection with Manila's market intact, seek submission through ordinary diplomacy, or use a diplomatic play to pursue direct rule. A qualifying conflict already underway can outlast the deadline.

**Who Shall Rule the Eastern Sea?** opens after final renunciation or the end of mainland mobilization. Build support for a republic, a Zhu monarchy with a Zheng cabinet, or a Zheng monarchy. Ratification opens a follow-up that requires six months of stable government under the chosen settlement.

**The Return to the Mainland** opens on reassertion or after Tungning gains a coastal foothold. Directly own all ordinary land in Fujian, Guangdong, or Zhejiang, clear enemy occupation, and reach 50% incorporation in that state to register automatically. Staff Government Administration and use local decrees to ease incorporation across the mainland core.

**The Reunification of China** opens once Tungning directly holds all three coasts. Use the journal map to check the rest of the core, offer provincial charters through diplomacy, and settle the political capital and local policies. Proclamation requires a secure coast, a directly held and secure Nanjing or Beijing, and a complete core. Keeping the court in Taiwan does not require mainland incorporation.

**The Enemy of Our Enemy** opens if the Taiping Rebellion forces a choice on the mainland claim. If Tungning has already reasserted, use the audience decision during the rebellion to open it voluntarily. Negotiate for coastal recognition and trade, then authorize aid separately. Holding all three coasts can unlock a larger bargain for the southern interior. Cessions require verified aid, a Taiping victory, and land the Heavenly Kingdom can legally transfer.

The government chosen in the constitutional debate speaks for the union. A republican assembly favors a local assembly for Taiwan; under Southern Ming the Zheng First Minister brings the case of the Estates or Maritime Houses to the floor. A Zheng court gives more weight to keeping the seat in Taiwan. You can choose either policy under any constitution, and proclamation leaves the crown, cabinet, and capital in place.

**Ming** is available under Nation Formation to independent countries with Han, Hakka, Min, or Yue as a primary culture and at least 60% of the 26 eligible mainland regions. Tungning cannot form it after permanently renouncing the Ming claim. A monarchy becomes Great Ming when it holds Beijing, or Restored Ming when it holds Nanjing without Beijing. The Republic of Ming and People's Republic of Ming have their own flags. Tungning has separate flags for its Catholic kingdom and its republic.

The mod patches Taiwan's buildings after the vanilla regional files. Taiwan starts with a level 3 port and no banana plantation. Tungning takes ownership and opens its level 2 trade center before the game assigns the starting workforce. The trade center starts with tea and Luxury Clothes exports and iron imports. With [Grey's Urban Synergy Unleashed](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3371689443) enabled, Taiwan's urban center starts with Horse-drawn Vehicles. Local financial districts own the new commercial buildings. Mods that also change Taiwan's starting buildings can overlap with this patch. The mod adds law variants and adjusts the vanilla rules needed to support them, including the Church and State group. Mods that override that law group need a compatibility patch. Tungning's setup is skipped if Qing no longer controls all of Taiwan when the starting economy is initialized.

If you're checking a new campaign, start as Qing and try the switch. While still paused at the opening date, check that the barracks are staffed for all 15 battalions: five marines, eight line infantry, and two cannon artillery. Tungning starts with ten early troopships and five frigates. Qing has three frigates. Check that Busan appears as an **active treaty port** in the Korean market. Advance through the first week before checking for Sintang, Selangor, and Bulungan, since their eligibility depends on the starting ranks. Save and reload to check that the opening choice and grants do not repeat.
