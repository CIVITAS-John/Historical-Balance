# The Return to the Mainland

The first task is to establish a government on the mainland that can survive the next war. Fujian, Guangdong, or Zhejiang can serve as that foothold. Holding one is enough to begin the larger campaign.

## Opening

- **Entry:** `fh_je_mainland_foothold`. Open on recorded reassertion, including through the [Taiping fork](08-taiping-rebellion.md#the-last-chance), or when Tungning first meets the [ownership rule](01-mainland-claim.md#mainland-foothold). If an unsettled claim resolves through ownership, record reassertion before opening this entry. While the Taiping fork awaits an answer, record any foothold gained but hold this entry closed until the court reasserts.
- **Existing settlement:** Ordinary renunciation does not sponsor a mainland campaign, but a later qualifying foothold can still open this entry without reviving Ming restoration, starting mobilization, or changing a Qing treaty. Permanent abandonment at the Taiping fork bars this entry entirely.
- **Display:** Show all three possible footholds with the outstanding ordinary land and its current owners. Losing land updates the display; the journal stays open.

## Mobilization after reassertion

- **Starts:** Apply the three-year [reassertion package](01-mainland-claim.md#reassert-the-claim) once when the court chooses Reassert, including when a foothold forces that choice. During mobilization, give AI Tungning more weight for barracks and their military supplies.
- **Ends:** Remove the package after three years or at the end of the first war in which Tungning fights Qing or its successor central government, whichever comes first. Count a war already in progress at reassertion and a war whose peace settlement forces reassertion. If Tungning reasserts at the Taiping fork while the rebellion is active, also end mobilization when that rebellion ends; fighting or actually delivering pact aid counts as following through. Answering the fork after the rebellion ends starts ordinary mobilization.
- **No war:** If the package ends without qualifying action, fire **A War Never Fought** once: −5 base legitimacy for one year, and −3 Army and Fleet approval if its leader is mainland-first at expiry. A backdown or a signed but unfulfilled pact does not count; a war still active at expiry does.
- **Defeat:** If the first tracked war ends in defeat for Tungning, apply −5 base legitimacy for one year. This can still happen if the three-year mobilization expired during that war.
- **Next journal:** Open the [constitutional debate](05-constitutional-settlement.md#opening) when mobilization ends. The no-war event does not extend the wait.

## Decision: Press the Mainland Claims

- **Available after Reassert:** Target one eligible owner at a time and assemble Return State demands for its claimed ordinary holdings in Fujian, Guangdong, and Zhejiang. This can target Qing or an eligible mainland successor that actually holds the land. Skip territory already recovered, treaty-port enclaves, and ordinary holdings of unrelated foreign powers. Suspend the preset play against Taiping while the wartime pact is in force; use its settlement process.
- **Diplomacy:** Only the preset coastal demands carry no infamy. Use normal play and settlement phases; check truces, existing conflicts, subject restrictions, interests, and eligibility. Show the target and demands before confirmation and recalculate them if control changes. Keep this action available in the reunification entry for unresolved coastal claims.
- **AI:** Use the decision only with at least 10 regular battalions, no default, a manageable opposing coalition, and either the target already fighting another war or at least 25% turmoil in one of its eligible target states.

## Consolidate the foothold

Choose **Register the Mainland Administration** once any one qualifying coastal region has two staffed levels of Government Administration, no enemy occupation, and **at most 20% actual turmoil at three consecutive monthly checks**. Progress resets if Tungning loses its full ownership, staffing, security, or calm before the third check. The other two regions carry over into [The Reunification of China](07-reunification-of-china.md).

The action opens a confirmation to enter the local rolls or require a common format. If several regions qualify, use the first in Fujian, Guangdong, Zhejiang order. Remember that state and recheck its readiness on confirmation. If it no longer qualifies, drop the choice and wait for the action to become available again. The AI uses the local rolls unless the School of Practical Learning is in government and the state has at most 10% turmoil. Both choices preserve earlier magistrates’ agreements.

Record completion once, apply the chosen [registration aftermath](outcome-modifiers.md#return-to-the-mainland), and open reunification and the [capital discussions](07-reunification-of-china.md#where-the-government-sits). It does not incorporate the state, move the capital, or grant more land. If Tungning later loses every foothold, the completed entry stays closed and reunification pauses national actions until one is recovered. Legal coastal recovery plays remain available. The original claim still settles on ownership alone; it does not wait for this administration to be ready.

## Settle the newly held states

The first qualifying coastal acquisition opens **The Magistrates at the Landing**. Later acquisitions appear in a journal list with their current turmoil, staffing, settlement, and remaining obligations. Use the same rules for every newly held ordinary coastal state and, once reunification opens, for directly acquired [core states](07-reunification-of-china.md#opening-and-progress) inland. Land taken through war, integration, or a Taiping cession enters the list with its actual turmoil; do not add a second automatic conquest shock.

| Settlement | Local recovery | Fiscal effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bargain with the local elites** | Keep county magistrates and recognize existing land registers and local offices. While unoccupied and over 20% turmoil, neutralize radicals equivalent to up to 1% of the state workforce each month, capped at the amount above 20%. Reduce new radicals from political movements by 25% for the term. The Ming Estates favor the bargain. | **−50% local tax capacity for four years**, even if the state becomes calm earlier. |
| **Send Taiwan commissioners** | Build a common civil service with help from the School of Practical Learning. With two staffed administration levels and no occupation, neutralize radicals equivalent to up to 0.25% of the workforce each month, capped at the amount above 20%. | No concession on tax capacity. Apply −5% state construction efficiency for two years while offices are reorganized; the monthly recovery effect has the same term. |
| **Leave the offices as they are** | Use normal laws, viable jobs, and standard turmoil recovery. This is the default until a settlement is chosen. | Normal administration and taxation. |

Use a state-scoped radical reduction for both agreements. This lowers real turmoil and represents the population’s willingness to live under the new government. It does not change cultural or religious legal acceptance, add loyalists, or hide turmoil penalties. Key the choice and expiry to the Tungning country and region; apply it only to the mainland state it directly owns, excluding treaty ports and land Tungning merely occupies. No renewal or switch while a term is running. Loss removes effects immediately; recovery within the term resumes the same deal for the time remaining, without a new reward or reset. An expired regional agreement cannot be reopened; later trouble uses normal recovery and resettlement.

Recheck eligibility before applying a magistrates reply. Defer it if there is no eligible coast. Show the terms without requiring the player to wait for the event: a calm, staffed state can qualify through normal recovery. Tungning’s AI prefers a compact for severe turmoil if its finances can take the tax cost; consider commissioners first if the state is already staffed and the treasury is under pressure.

## Resettle displaced families

Open **Fund the Resettlement Office** once Tungning holds a qualifying full coastal region during the mainland campaign. Keep it available as a decision even after reunification while Tungning holds at least one of those regions. The office pays for passage and reception at a named directly held destination. Projects run for up to **one year**, one at a time nationally, with a minimum **12 months between starts**. Charge **5% of the gold reserve cap from cash** on a successful launch and apply −5% available bureaucracy while the project is active. The Maritime Houses supply berths, the School of Practical Learning organizes a civil register, and the Catholic Missions can provide relief while they represent the Devout. Neither faith nor conversion is a condition of receiving help.

| Route | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Sponsor refugees from abroad** | Reuse the existing Fujian, Guangdong, and Zhejiang routes and their Min, Yue, and Han cultures. Require a valid coastal origin of at least 500,000 residents, at least 100,000 members of the route culture, and at least 15% turmoil. Select an eligible Tungning port instead of rolling for one. When the current origin and destination pass the existing mass migration rules, launch one native mass migration into that port. It draws from the origin country’s eligible Pops, as the existing event does. Share its four-year origin-region cooldown and country/culture conflict checks. If either border is closed, the countries share a market, or a stream is already active for the route, the funded action does not offer a workaround; the existing small, unsponsored escapes continue under their own rules. |
| **Evacuate civilians or help families return** | Use a domestic route between a Tungning-held strait-coast district and another district Tungning directly owns in the mainland core, Taiwan, or an established southern holding. For evacuation, require at least 20% turmoil in the origin. For return, offer passage to Min, Yue, Han, or Hakka families in Taiwan or a southern holding with at least 5% unemployment, provided the recovered mainland receiver is ready. Transfer parts of eligible lower-strata, non-enslaved Pops with the native partial-pop effect. Move no more than **10,000 people including dependents per wave** or 2% of the destination population, whichever is smaller. Allow up to three waves at least three months apart. Keep existing culture, religion, and political status. The offer represents families taking up passage; the existing events do not track individual refugee histories. |

Domestic receivers need at most 10% turmoil, less than 5% unemployment, and at least 80% market access. A shipping route needs working ports; a land route needs a contiguous connection in Tungning. A southern receiver must have finished its separate journal if one governs it. In both routes, exclude occupied land and treaty ports at either end, and exclude subject holdings and the origin state as destinations. Foreign arrivals must also pass the original destination checks. Recheck ownership, capacity, and route before launch and each domestic wave. Pause or end a domestic project whose route becomes invalid; a native stream already started can run through its ordinary rules. Use a one-year per-region cooldown for domestic starts at either endpoint, shared in both directions; do not start on an endpoint already participating in a native mass migration. Give no repeat starts, effects, or cost on reload. Close with one notice giving the route and results; never promise a fixed headcount for native migration.

A funded journey can take strain off a troubled state or bring workers back to an eligible one. It does not directly remove radicals or complete the pacification requirement; arrivals may bring their political grievances with them.

## Static checks

- A full region and a region with only a foreign treaty-port enclave outstanding each qualify.
- Guangdong qualifies without Macao while Macao remains a treaty port. If Macao becomes an ordinary foreign holding, mark Guangdong incomplete.
- An ordinary province outstanding, ownership of only a treaty port, occupation, and ownership by a subject each fail.
- Check the same opening after reassertion, after final renunciation, and while Qing’s offer is pending.
- Check mobilization when a Qing war ends early, when war continues past expiry, when Qing backs down before war, and when a peace transfer forces reassertion. Verify that only the no-war case triggers the penalty and that each case unlocks the constitutional debate once.
- Check the decision with no eligible targets, split ownership, and a truce. Verify that only the preset demands avoid infamy. Vanilla reference: the Balkan War button and play in `common/scripted_buttons/00_balkan_wars_buttons.txt` and `common/diplomatic_plays/00_diplomatic_plays.txt`.
- Check loss before answering the magistrates, simultaneous acquisition of all three regions, registration with only one region, and loss and recovery after registration. Verify that the recap and modifiers do not repeat on reload.
- Trace native unrest, three calm checks, interrupted staffing or occupation, and the two local settlements. Check that actual radicals fall only while eligible, that the tax cost survives calm and reunification, and that losing, splitting, or recovering a state does not duplicate a package or reset its term.
- Trace sponsored foreign migration, the same-market and Closed Borders cases, domestic evacuation and return, and loss or occupation of an endpoint between waves. Check that a failed launch spends no funds, that neither route duplicates Pops, and that waves and cooldowns persist across reloads.
- Vanilla references: `map_data/state_regions/11_east_asia.txt` (regions) and `common/treaty_articles/14_treaty_port.txt` (treaty-port status).
