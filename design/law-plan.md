# Qing, Tungning, and Sinosphere law plan

This proposal compares each law with Victoria 3 1.15 and identifies what actually changes in play. The 1836 changes for Đại Nam and Tungning’s industry and treaties are a separate delivery; the remaining law and journal changes are for review.

## Proposed laws against vanilla

The table shows the complete differences. Each variant retains its parent’s other effects and restrictions unless stated otherwise. Acceptance numbers are points; education access changes are percentage points.

| Law or amendment | Vanilla counterpart | Proposed difference and availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Standardized Examinations** | **Appointed Bureaucrats:** +50% bureaucrat political strength, +25% tax capacity. No education, bureaucracy cost, or research effect. | Keep those bonuses. Add **−5% population bureaucracy cost**, **−5% technology spread**, and **−3 points of education access plus 0.25 points per wealth level**. At wealth 5, 12, and 20 the net changes are −1.75, 0, and +2 points. Wealth is the game’s proxy here for middle and upper class access; literacy changes over time. Đại Nam starts with this instead of Hereditary Bureaucrats, giving up that law’s −25% population bureaucracy cost and +25% aristocrat political strength. Qing and Tungning would also start with it. Any country with a primary culture in the Sinosphere tradition can enact it, including Joseon. |
| **Yangban Administration** | **Hereditary Bureaucrats:** −25% population bureaucracy cost, +25% aristocrat political strength. Joseon actually starts with **Appointed Bureaucrats**. | **−30% population bureaucracy cost**, **+35% aristocrat political strength**. Joseon starts with it and keeps its regency. It can pursue Examinations through normal enactment. |
| **Banner Estates** | **Serfdom:** +25% Landowner political strength, a 75% minimum subsistence share of incorporated arable land, a 50% minimum subsistence employment modifier, peasant migration limits, and restrictions on economic laws. | Qing starts with **+30%** Landowner political strength and a **65%** minimum subsistence land share. Keep the employment modifier, migration limits, economic restrictions, and associated subsistence production. More land can be developed without giving up the estates’ political grip. |
| **Tenant Farmers** | **Vanilla Tenant Farmers:** +25% Landowner political strength, a 25% minimum subsistence land share, and restricted internal peasant migration. | Tungning starts with the vanilla law in place of inherited Serfdom. It loses Serfdom’s mass migration block, minimum employment modifier, and economic law restrictions. |
| **Mandate of Heaven** | **Subjecthood:** +100 authority, 100 base primary culture acceptance, −25% assimilation, 30 homeland acceptance, and access under several forms of government. | **+5 base legitimacy**, **90** base primary culture acceptance, **−35%** assimilation. Keep the rest of Subjecthood and require Monarchy. Qing starts with it. The Southern Ming uses the same law after restoration if its register law is still active; otherwise it only unlocks the option. |
| **Banner Precedence** | **Plain Mandate of Heaven:** no preference among primary cultures, no officer bonus. | Qing alone starts with **+10 Manchu acceptance** and **+10% officer political strength**. Under the Mandate this makes base Manchu acceptance 100 versus 90 for other primary cultures, including Han. The officer bonus applies to all officers. Remove the amendment permanently at the start of the Taiping Rebellion diplomatic play against Qing; keep the Mandate and both primary cultures. |
| **Port and Village Registers** | **Subjecthood:** +100 authority and 30 homeland acceptance. | Tungning starts with **+75 authority** and **40 homeland acceptance**. This helps communities in their own homelands, including the Indigenous population in Taiwan. Keep Min, Han, and Hakka as primary cultures; neither conversion nor joining the court’s faith is a condition. |
| **Coastal Registers** | **Canton System:** Canton is the only licensed gateway, with a +50 Trade Center limit and −25% trade capacity. It also grants +25% authority, +15% tax capacity, and +75% leverage resistance, and reduces technology spread by 10%. | Amend the actual Canton System: raise the Trade Center limit bonus to **+60** and reduce the trade capacity penalty to **−20%**. The gateway, other law effects, embargo, and Closed Borders stay. Permanently remove the amendment when Qing directly owns all of Taiwan, even if Tungning survives elsewhere. Occupation or tribute does not count. If Qing has left Canton, leave its current trade law alone. |
| **Anping Trade Charters** | **Mercantilism:** +25% export advantage, −25% import advantage, 20% export tariff allowance, 50% import tariff allowance, 50% export and 20% import subsidy allowances. | Tungning starts with **+35% export advantage** and a **10% export tariff allowance**; keep the rest. |
| **Confucian Academies** | **Religious Schools:** per level, +10 education access, +20% conversion speed, and +10% Devout political strength; −20% school institution cost. Requires Rationalism and excludes Total Separation, State Atheism, and Serfdom. | Per level: **+8 education access, +5% qualifications, +5% Devout political strength**, and **no conversion bonus**. Keep the cost discount. Requires Rationalism and an official Confucian faith; Banner Estates is the sole Serfdom exception. Nobody starts with it. If the faith changes, move to Religious Schools where legal without losing school investment. If blocked by the new legal settlement, use Private Schools where legal, then Public Schools if compulsory education precludes No Schools, otherwise No Schools. |
| **Yongli Articles of Conscience** | **Freedom of Conscience:** +100 authority; +15% Devout political strength; official/nonofficial wages +5%/−5%; religious acceptance +20/+15/+5/0 for official faith, shared trait, shared trait group, or no match. | Tungning starts with this amendment on Freedom of Conscience. Change the last two acceptance tiers to **+10/+5** and the nonofficial wage penalty to **−2%**. Keep official privileges and conversion rules. The same protections extend to Catholics if the court later adopts an ancestral faith, and to ancestral communities while the court is Catholic. |

**Maritime Customs Offices** is dropped. No opening school law changes are proposed.

## The Faith of the Court

The [backstory](../storyline.md) has Zheng Kezang invoke a promise made in the name of the Yongli court while the Zhu princes retain their ceremonial rites. The [1650 letter from Empress Dowager Helena Wang](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kircher-100-Elena-Wang-letter.png) anchors the Catholic court connection; the Articles themselves belong to the alternate history. In play, they protect missions, temples, and other congregations without making every confession official.

Extend [The Faith of the Court](journals/02-court-faith.md). The 1836 Tungning court and a restored Ming court on the same country tag share the same debate. While it is open, lock enactment of the Church and State law group and repeal of the Articles. Retain the current debate and four faith outcomes, including the deadline and temporary conversion slowdown. Remove the State Atheism suspension and the open-debate State Religion and Total Separation branches that depend on changing the locked law.

Once the faith result is known, freeze the debate and open a 30-day settlement audience. The official faith and the church law are separate decisions:

| Settlement choice | Law after settlement | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Keep the Articles** | Freedom of Conscience with the amendment | Keep the wider protections under any of the four settled faiths. This is the default if the player lets the audience expire or the AI has no strong reason to change. |
| **Repeal the Articles** | Vanilla Freedom of Conscience | Preserve limited tolerance and restore the normal wage and acceptance differences. |
| **Establish the settled faith** | Vanilla State Religion, when eligible | Remove the amendment. State Religion gives +200 authority, +30% Devout political strength, official/nonofficial wages of +10%/−10%, and acceptance of +25/0/−10/−25. |
| **Separate church and state** | Vanilla Total Separation, when eligible | Remove the amendment. Total Separation gives +15 religious acceptance at all four tiers and no official wage preference, authority bonus, or Devout bonus. It blocks ordinary conversion and requires checking any Religious Schools conflict. |

Show only legal switches. Check their native prerequisites without the journal lock for display, then release the lock and check native law availability before committing; default to keeping the Articles if a switch has become illegal. Apply any church law change before setting the settled faith. Preserve the existing approval, Devout, and diplomatic outcomes by settled faith, and leave the Manila Compact intact. A forced external replacement such as regime change interrupts the debate without imposing a second law or religion. An ordinary voluntary or imposed enactment is blocked while the debate is open.

## Implementation and checks

- Define the law variants and English text using their installed vanilla parents. Activate the named starts from the 1836 bookmark. Apply individual amendments only to their matching active law. Use full state ownership and a monthly fallback for Taiwan; use the exact Taiping diplomatic play start for Banner Precedence. Remember both removals even if the player has switched away from the underlying law.
- Add the church group’s native `change_allowed_trigger` in a narrow group definition override, retaining the existing fields and forced regime-change behavior. Other mods that override the same group will need a compatibility patch. Guard amendment repeal separately. Update the faith journal and use its one settlement handler for the new choices; update [The Southern Court Reborn](journals/05-constitutional-settlement.md) to use the same tag and preserve a player’s different citizenship law.
- Trace the four faith outcomes with keep, repeal, legal or blocked switches, and timeout. Check that changing the dynasty cannot bypass the lock, that other countries keep their normal law options, and that the school reform preserves investment when schools remain active. Check outcomes for partial conquest, full ownership, tributary Taiwan, and the exact Taiping play. Verify parent effects, localization, permissions, and identifiers against installed scripts; use static checks only.

## 1836 setup changes

- Đại Nam starts with Examinations, independently of Tungning setup. If both markets can trade, the **Huế Supply Accord** binds for five years: Đại Nam grants Tungning trade privileges; Tungning supplies **5 artillery per week** and delivers **two frigates once**. The exported hulls are separate from Tungning’s five starting frigates. The binding period sets when either court may withdraw without the early withdrawal penalty.
- Formosa starts with **two levels of glassworks, two of logging camps, one tooling workshop, one artillery foundry, and one coal mine**. The glassworks continue to make porcelain; the foundry makes cannons alongside the separate arms industry. The sawmills supply wood and hardwood, and the workshops still depend on market inputs such as iron and dye.
- Add **5 porcelain per week** from Tungning to Spain in both branches of the **Manila Compact**. Spain remains the treaty counterparty; the optional deal with an independent Manila market is separate.
